# Resources for Funders

## Purpose

This tool helps funders assess the evaluation maturity of organizations building or implementing generative AI applications in the development sector. It is a ready-to-use adaptation of the Generative AI Evaluation Playbook for the Development Sector. Having a response that reveals the evaluation practices are underdeveloped does not mean the project should not be funded. It does however suggest that the applicant should close those gaps before being considered impactful, and before large scale public procurement.

***
